Deleting your Bird League account

There are two ways to delete your account and its data.

In the app

Open Bird League, go to the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. Confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

By email

Email eajacobson67@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting an account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — email, username, display name, bio, avatar, life list, and totals.
  • Every sighting you logged, including its location, notes, and timestamp.
  • Every photo and audio recording you uploaded.
  • Your friend connections, in both directions.
  • Your league memberships and your scoring rows in those leagues.
  • Your push notification tokens.

What is kept

Leagues you created remain, because they contain other players’ scores and history. Your membership and your own scores are removed from them. Nothing that identifies you is retained after deletion.
